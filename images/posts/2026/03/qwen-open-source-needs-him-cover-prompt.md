**Scheme**: A — 连接与断裂（开源网络中一条核心连接正在断裂）

## Prompt

A digital illustration showing an open-source ecosystem as a network visualization. At the center, a warm glowing git fork/branch symbol radiates connection lines outward to dozens of small luminous nodes scattered across a deep navy blue background (representing global developers and projects). One prominent, brighter connection line from the center is fracturing and dissolving into golden light particles, symbolizing a key person departing from the community. The remaining connections still glow but slightly dimmer. The overall mood is contemplative and bittersweet — loss but also the enduring legacy of connections already made. Color palette: deep navy blue background, warm gold/amber for the central symbol and fracturing line, cool blue-white for the network nodes. Style: clean digital illustration with subtle particle effects, 16:9 aspect ratio. No text, no faces, no identifiable people. Abstract and symbolic.
