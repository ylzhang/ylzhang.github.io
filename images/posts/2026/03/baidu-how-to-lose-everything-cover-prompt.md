# Cover Image Prompt

**Article**: 百度是怎么把一手好牌打烂的
**Style**: Bold editorial illustration
**Concept**: 城堡 vs 出海 — 百度建了一座技术城堡，MiniMax 驶向了广阔的海洋

## Prompt

A bold editorial illustration in 16:9 ratio. On the left side, a massive, ornate fortress/castle made of circuit boards and tech components, with extremely high walls, deep moats, and many towers — but the castle is empty and grey, slightly crumbling, with cobwebs. Multiple flags on the castle towers bear product logos/names that are fading. On the right side, a vast bright ocean stretches to the horizon under golden sunlight. A small, sleek sailboat with a red sail is heading confidently toward the open sea, leaving a trail of light on the water. Small colorful flags from different countries flutter from the boat's mast. The contrast between the grey, overbuilt fortress and the bright, open ocean is stark. Style: bold editorial illustration, high contrast, striking composition, limited palette of grey/slate for the castle and vibrant blue/gold for the ocean. No text in image. No realistic human portraits. No brand logos.
