# 封面图 Prompt

## 方案：分岔路口

一个技术领袖站在分岔路口的剪影，前方是四条路径分别象征不同选择：大厂（高楼剪影）、学术（象牙塔）、独角兽（他人皇冠）、创业（敞开的光之门）。创业之路通向最明亮处。色调：思辨深蓝 + 金色希望感。风格：现代极简插画，扁平化设计，适合技术博客封面。

## 英文 Prompt

A silhouette of a tech leader standing at a crossroads, facing four paths symbolizing different choices: big tech (skyscraper silhouette), academia (ivory tower), unicorn (crown on others), and startup (open door with light). The startup path leads to the brightest destination. Color palette: deep thoughtful blue with golden hope accents. Style: modern minimal illustration, flat design, suitable for tech blog cover, 16:9 aspect ratio.
