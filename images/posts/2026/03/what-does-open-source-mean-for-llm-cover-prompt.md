# Cover Image Prompt

**Article**: 大模型的开源，到底开源了什么
**Scheme**: A — 分层解剖型（大模型被切开展示四个层次）
**Style**: clean digital illustration, warm-to-cool gradient

## Prompt

A clean digital illustration showing a large language model as a cross-section diagram, like a geological stratigraphy or an architectural cutaway. The model is visualized as a tall, glowing rectangular structure being sliced open to reveal four distinct layers from top to bottom:

Top layer (brightest, fully visible, warm gold): "Weights" — represented by flowing streams of luminous numbers and neural network nodes, fully exposed and accessible.

Second layer (visible, blue-white): "Inference Code" — clean lines of code flowing horizontally, well-lit and organized.

Third layer (partially obscured, dimmer blue): "Training Code" — similar code patterns but behind a semi-transparent barrier or frosted glass, partially visible but not fully accessible.

Bottom layer (hidden, deep navy with a subtle lock icon): "Training Data" — vast streams of text, images, and data flowing in darkness, behind a solid barrier.

The overall composition suggests "peeling back layers" or "looking inside." A warm amber light illuminates from the top, gradually fading to cool darkness at the bottom. The style is modern, clean, slightly abstract — think technical illustration meets infographic. Color palette: warm gold/amber at top, transitioning through blue-white to deep navy at bottom. 16:9 aspect ratio. No text, no faces, no identifiable people. Abstract and symbolic.
