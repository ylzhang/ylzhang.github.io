# AI Hallucination Article Summary Design

## Article Analysis
**Title:** AI幻觉背后的"洗车悖论"：当20米距离遇上逻辑黑洞
**Main Theme:** Exploring why AI makes logical errors like suggesting walking to a car wash

## Summary Diagram Design

### Diagram Title
**"AI Hallucination: When Distance Overrides Logic"**

### Stages (3-5 key phases)

1. **Statistical Prediction Engine**
   - Icons: Brain, database, probability graph
   - Description: AI calculates next token probability based on training data patterns

2. **Token Confusion & Pattern Matching**
   - Icons: Numbers 9.11, 9.9, version tags, maze
   - Description: AI breaks input into tokens, favors statistical patterns over logical meaning

3. **Missing World Model**
   - Icons: Empty brain frame, car icon, question mark, physical world symbols
   - Description: Lack of physical understanding leads to "walking to car wash" logic error

4. **Current Solutions**
   - Icons: Human feedback loop, chain of thought, search/document icons
   - Description: RLHF, CoT, and RAG approaches to reduce hallucinations

5. **Safety Implications**
   - Icons: Warning triangle, medical cross, gavel/law, financial chart
   - Description: Critical importance in medical, legal, and financial applications

### Flow Direction
Left-to-right horizontal flowchart

### Visual Style
- Hand-drawn/sketch-note style
- Light cream background
- Limited color palette: orange, blue, green, yellow, gray
- Minimal text: only titles and stage labels
- Arrow connections between stages
- Decorative elements: stars, curves, dashed lines

### Text Summary (2-4 sentences)
"AI hallucination occurs when statistical pattern matching overrides logical reasoning. Without physical world understanding, AI prioritizes common associations (20m = walk) over contextual logic (car wash requires car). Current solutions include human feedback, explicit reasoning chains, and retrieval augmentation. Critical applications demand human oversight despite AI's statistical prowess."
