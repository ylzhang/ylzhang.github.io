# 封面图 Prompt — life-main-quest

## 文章
《普通人的主线任务：前半生攒子弹，后半生造机枪》

## 视觉方案
隐喻型：从"攒子弹"到"造机枪"，表现普通人从原始资本积累到搭建个人系统的主线任务。

## Prompt
Minimal flat illustration, 16:9 horizontal composition, left to right progression. On the left, a lone figure in dim navy darkness stacking golden coins into a pile, symbolizing ascetic early capital accumulation. On the right, the same coins transform and assemble into an automated machine with turning gears that runs independently, glowing with warm golden light, symbolizing a self-sustaining personal system. A subtle light beam connects left to right through the middle. Deep navy blue background on the left gradually brightening to warm gold on the right. Clean lines, limited palette, editorial illustration style, intellectual and contemplative mood. No text, no realistic portrait, no brand logo.

## 风格
minimal flat illustration, clean lines, limited palette
