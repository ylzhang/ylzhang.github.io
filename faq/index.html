About Me#

Hi, I’m Lonney .Z — an author, engineer, and investor writing at the intersection of AI, technology, and markets. This blog is where I keep notes, test ideas, and document what I’m learning in public. I care about systems, incentives, and long-term compounding. The occasional math-heavy post is a fair warning: I enjoy going deep.

I’m broadly optimistic about technological progress. My core belief is simple: technology changes how society is organized, and the most interesting work happens where ideas become useful systems — products people use, organizations that adapt, and capital that backs the future before it is obvious.

Frequently Asked Questions#

About This Site#

What does this blog cover?

The site follows what I actually write about: AI and technology, paper notes and learning series, industry and policy commentary, and investment or macro themes. Everything here reflects my own reading, work, and research; no institutional endorsement is implied.

How often do you publish?

I aim for consistency rather than a fixed schedule. Posts usually come in clusters when I finish a reading project or have a clear view to share. You can subscribe via RSS at the bottom of the page or follow the site for updates.

How is the content produced?

I often draft with help from an AI assistant, then revise, fact-check, and edit the final version myself. I take responsibility for the published text and try to keep the reasoning grounded and the sources traceable where it matters.

May I cite or share your work?

Yes. Please attribute clearly, and keep the original wording intact when quoting.

About Education & Career Path#

Why did you go back to school in 2021 after graduating in 2013?

Learning does not have to follow a standard timeline. After years of engineering work, I became genuinely interested in management and economics, so I went deeper. If you find something you care about, I think it is worth taking seriously. There is no single “right” path; there is only whether you act on what you want to learn.

Why Connect with University Students?#

Why do you focus on linking undergraduates, especially at top universities?

Many capable young people are concentrated at leading universities — not exclusively, but in high density. What they often lack is early access to seed capital, industry mentorship, and research-style thinking. I have benefited from similar bridges myself. My goal is modest: use my experience in engineering, AI, and investing to help a few students move faster, whether toward a first company, a deeper research direction, or a clearer view of how technology and markets interact. I don’t claim to be uniquely qualified; I just try to be useful where I can.
